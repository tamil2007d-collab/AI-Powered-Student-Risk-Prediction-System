5 · SHAP — Explainable AI

The SHAP summary shows which features push probability toward at-risk (red) vs safe (blue) for every student. The waterfall plots decompose a single prediction: the base value is the population average log-odds, and each feature either adds or subtracts from it to reach the final probability. The high-risk student (P ≈ 1.000) is driven mostly by poor Teacher_Feedback, low Attendance, low Final, low GPA and high Late_Submissions; the safe student (P ≈ 0.000) by the reverse.

6 · Interpretation & Recommendations

  • Attendance and Teacher_Feedback are the strongest single indicators of risk — early-warning flags should watch these first.
  • Late_Submissions has a strong positive coefficient: frequent late work strongly increases predicted risk.
  • Final & GPA correlate heavily with risk, but they arrive late in the term — attendance, LMS engagement and quiz scores give earlier signals.
  • For high-risk students the app recommends: Meet Mentor, Parent Meeting, Extra Assignments, Weekly Monitoring.
  • For medium-risk: Weekly Practice, Improve Attendance, Join Study Groups. For low-risk: Continue Performance, Advanced Learning.

7 · Ethics & Fairness

Predictions are decision support: probabilities + SHAP explanations, never automated verdicts. Class-level precision/recall are surfaced so skewed behaviour can be audited. Always combine with human judgement before any conversation with a student or family.
